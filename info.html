Frequently asked questions

How do I book an event?

Open Events, press Book Now, pick the number of tickets and add them to your cart. Complete the order from the cart.

How do I pay for an order?

Orders are paid on delivery (cash or card). Tickets are delivered to your email after the order is confirmed.

Can I change the size of a product?

Yes. Choose the size on the product page before adding it to the cart, or remove the item from the cart and add it again.

Do I need an account?

You can browse without one. Create an account to keep your cart and orders together.

Help center

  • Orders: view or change items from your cart at any time before checkout.
  • Account: log in or sign up; use the header button to log out.

Terms of use

EntXHub is a student portfolio project. Trailers link to their official sources; posters and game artwork belong to their respective owners and are shown for demonstration only. Orders and bookings made on this site are demonstrations and are not charged.

Privacy

Your account, cart and orders are stored only in your own browser (local storage). Nothing is sent to a server, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser data.
